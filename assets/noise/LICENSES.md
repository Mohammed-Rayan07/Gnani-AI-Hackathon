# Noise beds

By default every bed is **synthetic**: generated in `chakravyuh/channel.py` (room, fan, traffic with horns, market babble made from modulated noise with no real speech).

To use recordings, drop `<kind>*.wav` files here (`traffic_1.wav`, `market_2.wav`, `fan.wav`, `room.wav`) and list each one below with its source and licence. Only use CC0 recordings, or your own recordings with **no bystander speech**.
